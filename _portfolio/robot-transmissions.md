When a geared actuator reverses direction, clearance between its gears can let the input move before the output responds. Our gearbox research addressed how to reduce this lost motion, called backlash, while keeping the transmission efficient.

A separate study investigated a transmission with an actively controllable reduction ratio. The prototype used a spring compression mechanism and could vary its transmission ratio from 1:1 to 4.2:1. This range lets a designer trade output speed for torque as the load changes.
